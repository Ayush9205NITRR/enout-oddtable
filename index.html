VII. Discretion first

11 CXO's,zero unknowns.

A table only works if everyone can speak plainly. So we verify seniority, keep every conversation off the record, and remove anyone who tries to trade on the room.

Verified

Seniority verified, every seat

Every member is verified against a current CXO, founder or board-level mandate before their first table. No observers, no plus-ones, nobody working the room.

Verified

Chatham House, always in 11

What is said at the table is yours to use; who said it is not. Eleven people, a private dining room, never a stage and never one-on-one. The format is the safeguard.

Verified

No selling, no exceptions

Pitching, recruiting or raising at the table ends the membership that night. One email to us and we act — the room is worth more than any single seat.

Verified

Conflicts screened before the drop

We check for direct competitors, live deals and reporting lines before a table is built. You'll always know the shape of the room before you say yes.

VIII. Honest answers

The questionspeople ask.

Q01Is this a networking club?+

No. Eleven people at one table, no badges, no round-robin introductions, nobody working the room. Business gets done sometimes. It's not the point.

Q02How much does it cost?+

It's free. You just pay for the meal. We ask for a 5000 rupee deposit which is refunded/adjusted against your bill as long as you show up on time.

Q03Why invite only+

It's easy to call yoyrself a CXO but we weed out the ones who are early on in their journey so that the conversations in the room are relevant for all involved. We might run a different version later for others who deserve a table of their own.

Q04What if I am running late?+

It's all CXOs like you at the table, and you know the value of your time. Multiply that by 10, and you will know why we don't expect or accept any late arrivals.

Q05Will someone be hosting or is it DIY?+

Hosted and managed. you have to worry about nothing except showiung up

Q06Why this, why now?+

This came from CXOs as a pain point, as our founders work with CXOs on a daily basis. It's an experiment we hope will run well and for long.

Q07Why Gurgaon only?+

Starting in Gurgaon, with Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru, and Noida coming soon. More cities as each one fills.
